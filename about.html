About us

A small, personal wedding and event hire business

Lilahleo began with a simple idea: that the little details of a celebration deserve the same care as the big ones. We hire out mirrors, display stands, lawn games and fully bespoke pieces for weddings, kitchen teas, baby showers and bachelorette celebrations, each one looked after by hand between events.

Made with care

Every piece in our collection is chosen, cleaned and checked before it reaches your venue.

Fully bespoke

Names, dates, colours and finishes can all be customised for signage and styled pieces.

Personal service

We confirm every booking personally, so you always know exactly where things stand.

Let's plan your celebration together

Get in touch and tell us about your wedding, kitchen tea, baby shower or bachelorette day.
